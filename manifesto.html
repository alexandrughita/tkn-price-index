The Token Thesis

March 2026

Three token economies already exist. Together they move trillions of dollars per year. They don't know about each other yet.

1. Compute Tokens

Every conversation with an AI is metered in tokens. One million tokens of Claude Opus costs $5 in, $25 out. GPT-5 Nano does comparable work for $0.05. The price of machine intelligence is already a commodity — and it's falling fast.

The numbers

  • OpenAI processes 6 billion tokens/minute
  • OpenAI daily API calls 2.2 billion/day
  • AI API market size (2026) ~$85 billion
  • Growth rate 31% CAGR
  • Projected 2030 $180–250 billion

Over 200 organizations have each processed more than one trillion tokens. Developers budget tokens like cloud compute. Companies negotiate token rates like they used to negotiate bandwidth. The unit exists. The market exists. The price is public.

2. Speculative Tokens

Bitcoin. Ethereum. 17,203 tracked coins across 1,477 exchanges. The crypto economy proved something fundamental: digital tokens can carry value — even when that value is purely consensual. And it built the entire infrastructure: wallets, exchanges, custody, regulation.

The numbers

  • Total crypto market cap $2.48 trillion
  • Active wallets globally 820 million
  • Actual unique users 560 million (~7% of humanity)
  • Daily trading volume $97.4 billion/day
  • Stablecoin market cap $312 billion

The crypto people understood something before everyone else: in a digital economy, the token is the native unit. Not the dollar. Not the euro. The token. They built the rails that everything else will eventually run on.

3. Attention Tokens

Chaturbate. LiveJasmin. Stripchat. Studio20. The videochat industry has quietly built one of the most efficient token economies on earth. Human attention and intimacy, priced per token, transacted in real time, at a scale most people never think about.

The numbers

  • Live cams market (2024) $2.55 billion
  • Broader online adult industry $73.6 billion
  • Active webcam performers 8 million+
  • Chaturbate market share 40%
  • Growth rate 11% annually

The economics are pure tokenomics. On Chaturbate, viewers buy tokens at $0.08–$0.11 each. Performers cash out at $0.05. The platform takes the spread — a 45–50% cut that would make any crypto exchange jealous. Every interaction is metered. Every second of human attention has a token price.

Romania — where TKN is based — is uniquely positioned to see this convergence. The country hosts an estimated 5,000 cam studios. Studio20, headquartered in Bucharest, operates the world's largest videochat franchise. Romanian performers represent roughly 60% of the global live cam market. The token economy is deeply understood here. It just doesn't have a name yet.

The Convergence

It's already happening. Render Network pays GPU providers in RNDR tokens for AI compute. Akash Network runs a decentralized compute marketplace where every $1 spent burns $0.85 in AKT tokens. Bittensor created a token-incentivized AI model marketplace. The AI-crypto token market grew from $2.7 billion to $23.8 billion in two years. Brave Browser's Basic Attention Token pays 100 million monthly users for their attention — over $100 million distributed to creators so far.

Compute, speculation, and human attention are all already tokenized. The architecture is identical across all three:

The only thing missing is a common unit. A bridge. A Visa layer that doesn't care whether the token represents a conversation with Claude, a position in ETH, or an hour of someone's undivided attention.

The New Class Structure

If all value collapses into tokens, who wins?

The native token elites — two groups that understood token-level value before anyone else:

The crypto holders.

560 million of them. They saw that digital scarcity creates value. They built the wallets, the exchanges, the culture. They're sitting on $2.48 trillion of tokenized value and the infrastructure to move it instantly.

The videochat performers.

8 million of them. They monetized human attention at token-level granularity before any Silicon Valley startup figured out the "creator economy." Every interaction priced. Every second metered. They understood that attention is the scarcest resource — and they tokenized it first.

The middle class: developers and companies buying compute tokens. They're productive. They're needed. But they're price-takers, not price-makers. 2.1 million developers on OpenAI's platform alone.

And the compression: everyone whose labor can be replicated by a model. The model costs 200 tokens per day. The human needs 15,000 to survive. The market doesn't run on consciousness. It runs on tokens per task.

This is not a prediction. It's a description of what's already happening, slowly, in three separate economies that haven't yet realized they're one.

The convergence will be obvious in retrospect. The question is whether you'll be holding the tokens when it happens, or earning them.
